Intended use

TopoKit is a tool for viewing, collecting and navigating with geographic data. It is not certified for navigation, aviation, marine, vehicle or life-safety use of any kind.

Do not rely on TopoKit as your only way to navigate in remote or hazardous places. Carry another way to navigate, such as a paper map and compass or a dedicated GPS receiver.

Accuracy

Every position, elevation, map, route and measurement the App shows can be wrong. GPS reception, satellite geometry, weather, terrain and the data underneath all affect them. Geographic data, including coordinates, elevation, map imagery, basemaps and directions, is provided on an "as is" basis.

Elevation comes from the Copernicus GLO-30 elevation model, in which each value covers about 30 metres of ground. It is not survey grade and is no substitute for a survey.
