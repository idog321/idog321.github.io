What is TopoKit?

TopoKit is an offline mapping app I built for iPhone and Mac. The app lets you create detailed topographic maps, import your own maps and data, and organize everything into projects and layers. All your data stays private on your device, and everything works without cell service.

How the band works

One side shows a map of the whole trail. The other shows its elevation profile at the same scale. Slide the red marker to where you are on the map, then flip the band over. The marker shows the elevation at that spot.

Before you go

The souvenir band should never be used for navigation. The band's map is unofficial, hasn't been checked in the field, and isn't produced or endorsed by BC Parks or any other land manager. The Juan de Fuca Marine Trail is in the territory of the Pacheedaht First Nation.

At high tide, some sections of the trail are impassable. Check the Port Renfrew tide predictions and BC Parks' current advisories before you go, and always carry another reliable way to navigate.
