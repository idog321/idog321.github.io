Get TopoKit

Maps

TopoKit opens georeferenced GeoPDF and GeoTIFF files, but finding maps for a particular area isn't always easy. I've put together a list of places to get them across Canada, the United States, Australia, New Zealand, the UK and Ireland. It includes free and paid downloads, along with XYZ and WMS map services that you can add to TopoKit and save for offline use.

Each country starts with national map sources, followed by maps for individual provinces, states and regions. Each entry explains what's available, where to get it and whether it's free. I checked the sources in October 2026. For help adding them to TopoKit, see Raster overlays for files or Tile layers for map services.

Some services need an API key or limit offline downloads, so check each source's terms.

Scanned NTS paper maps, 1:50,000 and 1:250,000

Natural Resources Canada · GeoTIFF, GeoPDF for CanTopo · Free · Open Government Licence – Canada, commercial use allowed · Dataset record

Every printed NTS sheet at 1:50,000 and 1:250,000, scanned and georeferenced, from 1944 to 2005, plus the newer CanTopo sheets for about 2,200 northern areas; no longer updated.

How to download
  1. Download the sheet index, topographic_index_50k.kmz, and read the sheet number over your area, such as 092B05.
  2. Open the folder for that sheet under …/raster/topographic/50k/: the first three characters, then the letter, then the two digits (50k/092/b/05/).
  3. Download the canmatrix_geotiff.zip with the highest edition number (about 10 MB), or the cantopo_geopdf.zip when there is one. The 1:250,000 sheets are under 250k/092/b/.

  3. GeoTIFF: open …/datasets/177864/tif/, then the folder for the UTM zone (utm07 to utm11, or bcalb for BC Albers), then the same four-character folder, and download the zip. The .tif inside is the map.
