Built for privacy,
from day one.

TopoKit was built without an account and without a server. Your projects live on your device, and in your own iCloud if you turn on sync. Nothing you record is public by default, because nothing goes anywhere by default.

  • What touches the network: Apple's basemap, search and directions.
  • Elevation tiles you ask for.
  • Map servers you add yourself.
  • That's the whole list. Everything else stays on your device.

Made by a working GIS technician.

Hi, I'm Nikolay. I'm an outdoor enthusiast and GIS technician in British Columbia, Canada.

I built TopoKit because I wanted to take my data outdoors, and I couldn't find a mapping app that handled both raster and vector data, kept everything private, and didn't lock me into an expensive subscription. So I made one.

I use the app weekly: for data collection, backcountry trips on my own time, and any fieldwork that starts where cell service ends.

I'm constantly learning from my own experiences with TopoKit and always working to improve it. If you have an idea or something you'd like me to fix, I'm happy to look into it. Every message comes straight to me.
