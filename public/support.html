Support

Having trouble with TopoKit, or have a question? TopoKit is a one-person project and I read every message personally.

support@topokit.ca

Reporting a bug

Say which iPhone or Mac you were using, its iOS or macOS version, and the TopoKit version, which Settings → About TopoKit → Version shows. Then say what you did and what happened instead.

If TopoKit ran into an error, Send Logs to Developer, under Settings → Help & Feedback and in the Mac's Help menu, emails the diagnostic log. TopoKit asks first, because the log can include project names, file paths and coordinates from your work, and nothing goes until you send it. After a crash, TopoKit asks at the next launch whether to send the system's crash report, which holds the app and system versions and where the crash happened, not your projects or your locations.

Purchases

TopoKit is a free download. Creating and editing need the full app, unlocked by Monthly or by Unlock Forever. Your TopoKit in the manual covers what works without unlocking, the two plans, Restore Purchases, Redeem Code and what happens when a payment fails. Apple handles billing and refunds for App Store purchases. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

The manual covers every part of TopoKit on iPhone and Mac, and the release notes list what each version changed.
